How to install & use

1
Drag the purple button above into your bookmarks bar. If hidden, press Ctrl + Shift + B.
2
Open Mevrik, then click Maverick Pro from your bookmarks bar. A compact panel appears.
3
Chats tab: Active chats are auto-detected. Click Hold BN or Hold EN to start the 44-second hold cycle.
4
Greetings tab: Toggle Auto-Greet ON, set your two greeting messages, and new chats are greeted instantly.
5
After a page refresh, click the bookmark again — holds resume automatically and settings persist via localStorage.
